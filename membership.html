Benefits

What membership gets you

  • Digital membership card issued in your name for the season
  • Priority ticket access for high-demand fixtures
  • Club shop discount on kit, training wear and accessories
  • Members’ newsletter with squad news before it goes public
  • Member events including open training sessions and an annual forum
  • Voting rights on selected club consultations (Patron tier)
  • Name on the supporters’ wall at Pfupajena Grounds (Patron tier)

Membership tiers

TierPriceCovers
Supporter$10One adult, full season
Family$25Two adults and up to three children
Patron$100One adult, plus voting rights and supporters’ wall
Junior (U18)$5One young supporter, full season

Prices are in US dollars. Payment is arranged directly with the club by EcoCash, bank transfer or in person.
