HEAD COACH PHOTOGRAPH
=====================

Save the head coach's photograph in THIS folder, named exactly:

    head-coach.jpg

That is the photo of her in the green club tracksuit against the light wall.

Recommended: portrait orientation, at least 720 x 900 pixels, saved as JPEG
at around 80% quality so the file stays under about 250 KB.

The website already expects this file. The moment you drop it in, she appears
as the large feature card at the top of the Coaches & Staff page. Until then
the site shows the club crest in its place rather than a broken image.

To add her NAME, open  assets/js/data.js , find:

    { name:'Head Coach', role:'Women's First Team', ...

and replace 'Head Coach' with her name. Her role line stays as it is.

You can delete this text file afterwards.
