Community

Football changes lives. We make sure it does.

Our community programmes use football as the doorway into education, health and opportunity for young people across Chegutu district.

  • Keeping children in school
  • Girls’ empowerment
  • Drug and substance abuse awareness
  • Health education and youth leadership
  • Disability inclusion and football festivals
